The Barrier: Knowing vs. Doing

Explaining Georgian grammar rules is one thing; using them in fast-paced conversation is another. Most learners know the patterns on paper, but freeze mid-sentence trying to mentally calculate verb forms and case endings.

Native speakers don't calculate rules while talking—they rely on muscle memory. KartuPro is designed to replace endless mental calculation with active intuition.

The Training Court

Imagine learning basketball. Watching games isn't enough, and jumping into a match without basic skills is overwhelming. You build real skills through isolated, high-rep drills.

KartuPro is your training court for Georgian. We break down the hardest parts of the language into focused exercises:

Targeted Reps

Drill complex verbs and noun declensions until forms become automatic.

Context-First

Ear Training

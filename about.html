The Problem

Being able to explain Georgian grammar is a valuable skill, but it is NOT the same as being able to use it real time. Many of us struggle to actually use the grammar we know.

How Natives Speak

Think about your first language. Do you know how many tenses there are? Do you remember all their names? Can you recite all the precise nuances of how they work? Unless you are a teacher, the answer is likely no. But you have no problem speaking.

The Goal

Let's activate what we do know and practice it in targeted ways until it becomes second nature. Wouldn't it be great if we didn't have to pause every other word to "calculate" how a word should change in this sentence?

How it Works

  • Active Studying: Apply what you know. Real work. Real results.
  • Targeted Exercises: Focus on the specific areas that keep tripping you up.
  • Contextual Learning: Useful examples in real sentences, not just isolated words.

A Sports Analogy

Consider basketball. Many skills are involved in the sport: dribbling, shooting, passing, defense. Simply watching a video isn't enough; you need real practice. You can get this practice in a game, but often it's more effective to do targeted exercises to quickly improve in a certain skill.

Our goal is to help you avoid both traps. Instead of 'watching', you'll actively produce the language. Instead of being overwhelmed trying to learn during a 'game', we provide targeted exercises.

Then you'll be ready for game-time: real conversations with real people to build real connections. You'll utilize each of the skills and gracefully combine them all together.
